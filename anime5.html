Top 30 Anime Masterpieces

A curated definitive list of the greatest anime series of all time

#1

Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

Genre: Shonen, Fantasy Rating: 9.1/10

Two brothers use alchemy in an attempt to bring their deceased mother back to life, triggering a devastating chain reaction.

#2

Attack on Titan

Genre: Action, Dark Fantasy Rating: 9.0/10

Humanity fights for survival against giant man-eating humanoids known as Titans behind massive concentric walls.

#3

Steins;Gate

Genre: Sci-Fi, Thriller Rating: 9.0/10

#4

Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War

Gintama°

Hunter x Hunter (2011)

Legend of the Galactic Heroes

#12

Jujutsu Kaisen

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

#14

Vinland Saga

One Piece

Cowboy Bebop

Naruto Shippuden

#30
